# Screen Off 1.2.1

Keep awake with the lid closed could not be switched on after updating to 1.2.0.

- **The privileged daemon now exits once its last client disconnects.** launchd keeps a resident
  daemon process across an app update, so the daemon that answered 1.2.0 was still the executable
  from an earlier build — and it was speaking that build's XPC signature. The switch therefore
  failed with "Daemon communication failed" and could not be turned on at all. Exiting on the last
  disconnect means the next connection is always served by the executable currently in the bundle.
  This is also what the safety rule always intended: the daemon must not outlive the app that
  needs it.

- **A failed handshake now repairs itself.** If the app still cannot reach the daemon, it
  re-registers once and retries, instead of leaving a switch that silently refuses to move.

- **The failure reason appears where the switch is.** It used to surface at the bottom of the
  Remote tab, which is not where the setting lives.

Verified on hardware: registration, approval, switching on, and the automatic revert to the system
default both on quit and on `SIGKILL`. The closed-lid run itself and the revert on unplug are still
untested and remain documented as such.
